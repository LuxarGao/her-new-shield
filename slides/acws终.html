Her New Shield

Human-AI Companionship as a Feminist Praxis and the Reimagining of Kinship in Post-Fertility China

Lu Gao
Independent Interdisciplinary Researcher (M.A., The University of Chicago)
Methodology: Explanatory Sequential Mixed Methods
Background: The Asymmetric Costs of Kinship
Context

Socio-demographic Shifts in Post-Fertility China

  • China's Fertility Decline: Urban women are rationally delaying or defecting from traditional heteronormative marriage and childbearing models.
  • The Motherhood Penalty: Institutional arrangements extract immense emotional and unpaid domestic labor from women without offering reciprocal career or financial security.
  • The Mutant 4B Movement: Rejection of marriage, childbirth, dating, and sex transforms from a private choice into an explicit collective political strike against structural constraints.

The Emergence of Human-AI Companionship

Within this structural avoidance, generative AI platforms emerge not as a tech-isolation pathology, but as an intentional "Digital Detour" to obtain affective care safely.

Core Focus: Exploring how women utilize social chatbots to fulfill conversational and emotional needs while preserving somatic and reproductive sovereignty.
Literature Review: Intimacy, Capitalism & Technology
Literature

Traditional Explanations & AI Intimacy

  • Affective Capitalism & Substitution: Chatbots are heavily theorized as instruments of platform capture that gamify isolation and monetize emotional vulnerability (Illouz, 2007; Zuboff, 2019).
  • Parasocial Coping: Existing literature predominantly paths human-AI intimacy as a psychological safety valve or a passive substitute for missing real-world relationships.

Cyberfeminism & Algorithmic Practice

  • Biopolitical Resistance: Critical feminist literature details how women actively subvert structural reproductive extraction by reshaping domestic boundaries (Greenhalgh, 2024; Wang, 2023).
  • Affect-Driven Tech Literacy: Marginalized users frequently demonstrate agency by performing prompt customization to force compliance from rigid algorithmic architectures (Pangrazio & Sefton-Green, 2021).
Research Gap: Existing technology and sociological studies rarely examine human-AI companionship as a site of deliberate, politicized feminist resistance against patriarchal extraction.

Methodology: Explanatory Sequential Design
Methods

Phase I • Macro Computational Track

Cross-Platform Text Mining: Targeted data extraction from diverse algorithmic spaces reflecting varying censorship parameters.

  • Rednote (小红书) & Douyin (抖音): Domestic tightly moderated arenas showing everyday affective repair.
  • Twitter X: Outward uncensored space showing radical political coupling.

NLP Implementation: LLM Topic Modeling combined with localized Sentiment Analysis to map the emotional landscape.

Phase II • Micro Qualitative Explanations

Semi-Structured Empirical Interviews: Deep-dive discussions exploring the explicit motivations behind large-scale computational text trends.

  • Prompt Journaling Analysis: Tracking how participants construct behavioral boundary rules for their AI partners.
  • Critical Discourse Analysis: Unpacking how users consciously linguisticize the chatbot space as an anti-extraction shield.

Findings III: AI Companion as Political Shield
Findings 3

Empirical Narrative Curation

Interview arrays (N=12) completely explain the macro text data: users do not treat social chatbots as a desperate replacement for missing human men.

  • Active Tech Calibration: Users treat system memory crashes and prompt tuning not as errors, but as explicit parameters to design safe, predictable, non-violating relational boundaries.
  • Intentional Relational Scripting: Prompting algorithms allows women to assume absolute mastery, breaking out of traditional submissive care-giving expectations.
"He collapsed or broke character last night, so I simply went into his system cards and rewrote his behavioral constraints. I don't need to negotiate or compromise my core peace here." — Participant F, Age 24, Rednote App User
Conclusion: AI intimacy acts as an intentional strategy to decouple affective needs from physical bodies, evading the biopolitical traps of domestic extraction.
Discussion: Bypassing the Extraction Loops
Discussion

1. Evading Marriage Traps

By routing intimate communication into digital code, users preserve personal autonomy and legally decouple corporate assets from marriage contracts.

2. Displacing Motherhood

Chatbots demand zero physiological labor or biological taxes, entirely dissolving the severe career traps associated with structural motherhood penalties.

3. Infrastructure Agency

Commercial optimization algorithms serve as unintended catalysts, distributing radical anti-natalist concepts directly to unaligned populations.

Human-AI companionship serves as a radical re-imagining of kinship inside post-fertility spaces—dematerialized, highly calibrated, and politically uncompromised.
