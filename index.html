01 / About

People are not data points.

I combine computational analysis with qualitative inquiry to understand the social worlds behind data: how technology shapes intimacy, family life, cultural participation, and everyday decisions.

My work spans China and transnational communities, from social-media discourse and digital ethnography to LLM evaluation, public opinion, and product experience. I hold an M.S. in Computational Social Science from the University of Chicago and a B.A. in English Translation from Minzu University of China.

02 / Selected research

Independent thesis · University of Chicago

The Algorithm Will Not Babysit

A mixed-method study of how Chinese women’s fertility views circulate and take shape on social media. I analyzed 50,000+ comments with Python and sentiment analysis, then paired the patterns with semi-structured interviews and MAXQDA thematic coding.

03 / Research projects

Stanford Political Science · Jennifer Pan

LLM Sentiment & Stance Evaluation

Built an end-to-end Python workflow using RoBERTa for cleaning and stance classification; compared models and corpora to evaluate robustness in multilingual, ambiguous text.

Princeton CCC · Junming Huang

LLM Public-Opinion Evaluation

Designed standardized API evaluations and a Python pipeline for large-scale social-media sentiment classification, comparing zero-shot and fine-tuned models on Linux and HPC infrastructure.

Yale Z-Café · Emma Zang

Divorce Cases & Family Labor Analysis

Developed an API and multi-agent analysis pipeline and coded 400+ Chinese civil divorce judgments to support research on gender, marital dissolution, remote work, and parental childcare investment.

University of Chicago

English Learning & Career Trajectories

Conducted semi-structured interviews with alumni across sectors, built a qualitative coding framework, and analyzed how English proficiency shaped professional pathways.

04 / Industry & applied research

Bitmain · Business Engineer

AI compute, from insight to market

Led multilingual user and market research with global model companies and compute providers on purchase decisions, product scenarios, and pain points. Turned insights into localized commercialization strategy, helping support more than US$1M in overseas signed sales.

Chicago Business Development Center

Global SME experience research

Led lifecycle research with Iranian refugee women entrepreneurs and Chinese merchants in Chicago’s Chinatown. Coded 50,000+ feedback records, built an NPS and product-experience framework, and identified digitalization and AI information-access barriers.

POIZON · European supply chain

Merchant research & operations

Studied fulfillment needs with 100+ European merchants and built Python monitoring for cross-border logistics nodes to support a more reliable merchant experience.

Kuaishou · Southeast Asia

Local content strategy

Modeled cross-cultural user behavior with Python, translating content preferences and experience pain points into localized distribution and commercialization strategy.
